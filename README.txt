Zigbee Monitor keeps an eye on your Zigbee routers: the mains-powered lamps, plugs and relays that pass on the signals of your other Zigbee devices. Every few minutes it gives each router its current state again. Nothing changes, but the command really travels over Zigbee, so a router that stopped responding is noticed before you do. It works with routers of any app, such as Philips Hue without bridge, IKEA and Aqara.

When a router does not respond several times in a row, you get a notification and a flow can repair it: restart the app of that router with Homey's own Restart app card, test again, and if needed restart Homey. One overview device shows a red dot when a router does not respond.
